Upload a handwritten grocery list → extract the items → structure them with AI → search products → add them to an Instamart cart using MCP.

## What I built

A grocery ordering workflow that turns a handwritten list into structured shopping items and connects those items to an Instamart ordering flow.

## Flow

- Upload a handwritten grocery list.
- Extract and structure the items with AI.
- Search for matching products.
- Let the user review the selections.
- Add the approved products to the cart through MCP.

## Stack

Python, LLMs, OCR, MCP and APIs.
